CardsRG is a small, family-run card shop out of Miami. We buy what we'd collect ourselves, price it off real comps, and show up at the shows so you can see it in hand.

Our Story

An uncle, a nephew,
and way too many slabs

It started the way most collections do: a couple of packs, a few trades, and a shoebox that somehow turned into a display case. Jr and his uncle Leo turned that shared obsession into CardsRG, a two-person operation built on the cards we actually love: PSA-graded Pokémon and One Piece, soccer parallels, Marvel, and the occasional sealed box we couldn't leave behind.

Everything here is hand-picked and photographed by us, priced against recent sales, and shipped like it's going into our own collection. No warehouse, no middlemen. When you DM the shop, you're talking to family.

You'll find us behind the table at South Florida card shows most months. Come say what's up, rip some packs, and make a deal.
